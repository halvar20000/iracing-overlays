## Spoken script

Hook and CTA are written tight — say these close to word-for-word. The demo
sections are talking-point bullets on purpose: you present these best riffing,
not reading.

### 1. Cold open — before/after (0:00–0:15)  *[scripted]*

- Show 3–4 seconds of a RAW iRacing gameplay clip — no overlays, flat.
- Cut to the SAME race with all overlays running.
- Say over the cut:
  > "This is a normal iRacing stream. And this… is the same race with 13 free
  > overlays running on my PC. Let me show you — and how to get them."

### 2. The problem (0:15–0:45)  *[talking points]*

- A raw iRacing broadcast is hard to *watch*: viewers can't see the order, the
  gaps, who's closing in, who just spun.
- The tools that fix this are usually paid, cloud-based, or a headache to set up.
- "So I built my own — and made them free."

### 3. Meet the tool (0:45–1:30)  *[talking points]*

- Name it: **iRacing Overlays.** 13 separate overlays, each a small program on
  your PC, each added to OBS as a browser source.
- Fast highlight montage — a couple of seconds each:
  Standings · Track Map · Livery (car of whoever's on camera) · Flag · Results ·
  Live championship · Race logger charts.
- The line that matters: "No subscription, no account. All the telemetry stays
  on your machine — nothing gets uploaded anywhere."

### 4. In action — the meat (1:30–3:30)  *[talking points, riff freely]*

Pick your 4–5 favourites and narrate WHY each makes a stream watchable:
- **Standings** — live order, real gaps, brand logos, pit status. Updates the
  instant a pass happens, not at the line.
- **Track Map** — every car as a live dot, fully offline, ~300 tracks bundled.
- **Livery** — a 3D render + name of whoever the camera is on. Pure TV look.
- **Dashboard auto-replay** — it detects spins, contact and overtakes on its own
  and can auto-trigger a replay. This is your "wow, it does THAT?" beat — linger.
- **Race logger + live charts** — gaps/positions over the race, addable to OBS,
  and viewers can even open their own self-service chart via a share link.
- Mention in passing: there's also **iCASControl**, a full race-control /
  stewarding dashboard for league admins — "a whole video on its own."

### 5. How easy it is (3:30–4:30)  *[talking points]*

- Kill the "too technical for me" fear on screen:
  - Download from GitHub, one command to install.
  - Double-click **`launch_gui.bat`** — a little app with a Start button per overlay.
  - In OBS: add a Browser source, point it at the overlay (or the self-healing
    loader file so it never shows blank). Done.
- "You can have this running on your stream in about five minutes."

### 6. The AI angle (~30s)  *[talking points — your channel's whole identity]*

- "Here's the part I like most: I built all 13 of these with AI as my coding
  partner. This is what one person plus AI tools can ship now — and it's the kind
  of thing this channel is about."
- Plant the next videos: "I'll break down how I built these, and the league
  manager, in future videos."
- *(Phrase this in whatever way is honestly true for how you work — keep it real,
  it's more compelling than hype.)*

### 7. CTA / outro (last ~20s)  *[scripted]*

> "It's completely free — the download link is in the description, straight from
> GitHub. Grab it, try it on your next stream, and if it makes your broadcast
> better, subscribe — there's a lot more sim-racing-plus-AI coming. See you in
> the next one."

## Production notes

**Recut vs reshoot — my recommendation: hybrid.**
Your existing "just for me" recording is perfect *demo material* — reuse the
screen/overlay footage. But a discovery video lives on its first 15 seconds and
its pacing, which a personal recording rarely has. So: keep the gameplay/overlay
capture, record a fresh tight **before/after hook** and re-narrate to this
structure. Low effort, built to be found.

**EN + DE the cheap way.**
Record the screen demo once. Lay down the English voiceover, then a German
voiceover over the SAME footage → two uploads. But publish the **English one
first and on its own**, see if the format lands, and only cut the German version
once it shows traction. Don't double the work before you know it converts.

**Get the before/after clip.** The single highest-leverage asset is 3 seconds of
raw iRacing next to 3 seconds with overlays. Capture that deliberately.

**Next videos already implied:** "How I built these with AI", the CLS League
Manager, and a deeper iCASControl / stewarding walkthrough. This first video
plants all three.
